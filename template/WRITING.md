# Writing guidelines

## Voice

Clear, calm and specific. The voice stays the same everywhere; the tone adapts to the moment.

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Onboarding, empty states | Encouraging and direct: say what to do next |
| Errors | Calm and exact: what happened and how to fix it; no apologies |
| Destructive confirmations | Serious and explicit about what will be lost |

## Interface copy rules

- Buttons start with a verb and say what happens: "Save changes", not "Submit".
- An action keeps its name through the flow: "Publish" leads to "Published".
- Sentence case for all labels, headings and buttons.
- Error messages name the problem and the fix in one or two short sentences.
- Use numerals for numbers ("3 items").

## Terminology

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Sign in | Log in, Login |
| Delete | Remove (when the item is gone for good) |

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do write from the user's side: "notifications", not "webhook config".
- Don't use exclamation marks in errors.
- Don't use filler like "simply" or "just".
