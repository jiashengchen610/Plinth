# Switch

## Parts
- Root (role switch)
- Thumb

## States
Off, on, focus-visible, disabled.

## Props
Radix Switch props: `checked`, `defaultChecked`, `onCheckedChange`, `disabled`, `name`, `value`, `required`.

## Tokens
switch-track-on, -track-off; switch-thumb-on, -thumb-off; shadow-card; duration-fast; ring.

## Accessibility
Space toggles; announced as a switch with its state. Always labelled. The change takes effect at once.
