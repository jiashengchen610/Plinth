## What is here

- **Foundations**: the design tokens (colors, typography, shape, elevation, motion) and the design and writing
  guidelines. Every value comes from the token files in this repository.
- **Components**: every component with one story per variant and state, its props, and its accessibility tests.

Switch the **brand** and **light / dark** mode in the toolbar: everything on these pages follows.

This design system is maintained with [Plinth](https://github.com/jiashengchen610/Plinth). The token files, the
guidelines and the components are plain files in this repository, and each brand has a complete `DESIGN.md` in
`exports/` for other AI tools.
